What I Look At

The gap between your ad and your page

A paid ad builds a specific story: a problem, a mechanism, a reason to act now. When the landing destination does not immediately continue that story, the visitor arrives skeptical instead of ready. That gap is where revenue disappears.

Ad Angle

What specific story, claim, or hook the selected ad establishes in the first few seconds.

Landing Continuity

Whether the first screen of the destination carries that same angle forward or resets to generic messaging.

Offer Clarity

How quickly and clearly the product, price, and reason to buy appear above the fold on mobile.

Purchase Path

Whether a dedicated campaign page could remove friction without touching the main store or checkout.
