What I Look At

The gap between your ad and your page.

A paid ad builds a specific story. When the destination does not immediately continue that story, the visitor arrives uncertain instead of ready. That gap is quiet, invisible in your ad metrics, and expensive. I look at four things to find it.

01

Ad Angle

What specific story, claim, or mechanism the selected ad establishes in the first few seconds. What promise the visitor is carrying when they click.

02

Landing Continuity

Whether the first screen immediately carries that angle forward, or resets to generic product messaging that forgets the ad existed.

03

Offer Clarity

How quickly the product, price, and reason to buy appear above the fold on mobile, without the visitor having to scroll or hunt.

04

Purchase Path

Whether a dedicated campaign page could remove friction without touching the main Shopify store, product pages, or checkout in any way.
