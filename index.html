What I Look At

Where your paid traffic stops converting.

Most brands lose revenue not in the ad, but after the click. A strong offer falls flat when the landing experience does not continue the ad's story. I look at four things to find exactly where that breakdown happens.

01

Ad Angle

What specific story, claim, or hook the selected ad establishes in the first few seconds. What promise the visitor is holding when they click.

02

Landing Continuity

Whether the first screen immediately continues that angle, or resets to generic brand or product messaging that ignores the ad entirely.

03

Offer Clarity

How fast the product, price, and reason to buy now appear above the fold on mobile, without the visitor having to scroll or search for them.

04

Purchase Path

Whether a dedicated campaign page could remove friction and scale the offer, without requiring changes to your existing store or checkout.
